Cop Dodge: Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Cop Dodge (formerly Can't Catch Me, "the game") is a mobile game published by vlodargames ("we"). This page explains what information is handled when you play, who handles it, and what choices you have.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads and sells optional items. The companies that provide the ads and process the purchases receive some technical data from your device, described below.

Data saved on your device

Level progress, coins, unlocked cars, best times and settings (sound, music, view) are stored in the game's local storage on your device. We cannot see this data. It is deleted when you clear the game's data or uninstall the game.

Data handled by other companies

ServiceWhat it is used forData it may receive
Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) Showing ads, measuring them, preventing ad fraud Advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, how you interact with ads, diagnostic data
Google Play Billing Processing purchases Purchase details. Payment is handled by Google; we never see your card or bank details.
RevenueCat Confirming and restoring purchases A random app user identifier, purchase history for this game, device and app information, IP address

These companies act under their own privacy policies: Google, how Google uses data from apps that use its services, RevenueCat.

Ads and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your answer at any time with the "Privacy choices" button in the game's menu.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Buying "No ads" in the game removes the ad breaks.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

How long data is kept

We keep no personal data on our own servers. Data held by Google and RevenueCat is kept according to their policies. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to restore your purchases and to meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because the game has no accounts, most requests are handled by resetting your advertising ID or uninstalling the game. For anything else, write to us and we will help, including passing your request to the services listed above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
